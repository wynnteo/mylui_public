Features

Built for the SG–MY commuter

Most finance apps assume you live in one country. MyLui doesn't.

Multi-currency net worth in one view

SGD, MYR, USD, CNY, EUR, GBP — all converted at live exchange rates and totalled into a single net worth figure. Tap any currency to see everything in that denomination.

CPF and EPF side by side

Track your Singapore CPF and Malaysia EPF alongside your other accounts, all converted to your chosen display currency.

Property tracking

Log purchase price, outstanding loan, and monthly rental. MyLui calculates your equity and rental yield automatically.

SGX and Bursa investments

Add stock holdings from SGX or Bursa Malaysia. Live prices from Yahoo Finance. P&L and unrealised gains calculated per holding.

Financial goal tags

Tag accounts as Retirement, Emergency Fund, Education, or House. See how much you have allocated toward each goal at a glance.

Home screen widget

Your current net worth on your home screen. Swipe to cycle through accounts without opening the app.

Private by default

Everything stored on your device. No sign-up, no cloud sync. PIN or biometric lock included.

English and Simplified Chinese

Full localisation in both languages. Switch at any time in settings.

Analytics

Understand your money, not just count it

Update your balances once a month. MyLui handles the rest — trends, velocity, allocation, and year-over-year growth.

Privacy

Your data never leaves your phone

MyLui has no accounts and no servers of its own. Every balance, account, and investment you enter is stored in private storage on your device.

The only third party is Google AdMob, which shows banner ads in the free tier. MyLui Premium removes ads entirely.
